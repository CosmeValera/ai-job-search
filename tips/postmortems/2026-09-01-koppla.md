## Context

Construction scheduling platform, founded 2020 out of University of Potsdam / Hasso Plattner
Institute. €6M raised May 2025 led by Newion and Brandenburg Kapital, with Earlybird, Coparion and
HPI Ventures. Stack: TypeScript, Vue 3, Tailwind, NestJS, Prisma, PostgreSQL, AWS, Terraform.

Rejection came at the screening stage, **before any code was seen**. So this was decided on story and
fit, not on technical ability. Nothing technical was tested.

## What they asked

1. **"What culture do you expect from this company?"**
2. **"Why do you want to move?"** — then, when the answer was about Berlin: *"but why koppla
   specifically?"*
3. Standard background and motivation questions.

## What went wrong

**The culture question — the biggest miss, and it was avoidable.** The answer given was an improvised
guess about the company organising social events. That is a benefits answer, not a culture answer,
and interviewers hear the difference. The real problem was guessing at all: koppla publish a Culture
Playbook, and the posting itself already listed *transparency, respect, ownership, collaboration,
well-being of each team member*. The answer was printed on the page the application was submitted
from.

Genuine preferences were available and went unused — small team with sight of the whole product,
code review as real conversation, direct contact with domain-expert users (ESA engineers at GMV),
understanding why something is being built. Those would have overlapped their playbook honestly.

**The "why koppla" argument came out inverted.** The prepared thread was that GMV/Capgemini and
koppla are the *same* category: software coordinating physical, expensive things where delay costs
real money. What was actually said was the opposite — that GMV/Capgemini were physical-world and
koppla was a consumer app. koppla is construction software, so the logic broke against her own
product. Interviewer was visibly unconvinced, though not put off.

**Led with the wrong reason.** "Two brothers already live in Berlin" opened the answer. That is a
*why Berlin* reason, and it signals the city matters more than the company.

## What was not wrong

Rejection at round 1 of 4 in a 65–90K Berlin band, from a cold application, at ~4 years of
experience. Reaching a live process at that band is the funnel working. Likely outcompeted by
candidates further along in the same pipeline, on top of the two unforced errors above.

## Intel gained

- **Process is four stages:** HR screening → coding challenge → team interview → final interview
  with the CEOs.
- **Headcount ~20 → ~44 in one year**, raising again. This contradicts the *"we are not looking for
  hypergrowth"* line in the posting, and supersedes the ~20 figure from the May 2025 funding coverage.
